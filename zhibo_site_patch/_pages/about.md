I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics](https://science.psu.edu/astro) at **The Pennsylvania State University**, working with [Prof. W. Niel Brandt](https://personal.science.psu.edu/wnb3/).

My research focuses on the **growth of supermassive black holes (SMBHs)** and their connection to galaxy evolution. I use large multiwavelength surveys—from X-rays to the infrared—to study active galactic nuclei (AGNs), black-hole accretion, galaxy properties, and the physical processes that regulate SMBH growth across cosmic time.

A major theme of my current work is understanding **when, where, and how rapidly SMBHs grow**, and whether processes such as **galaxy mergers** play a major role in triggering black-hole accretion. I am particularly interested in combining deep and wide surveys with modern statistical and machine-learning methods.

My recent work includes studies of the decline of cosmic SMBH growth since cosmic noon, dust-obscured galaxies in XMM-SERVS, and the dependence of black-hole growth on galaxy stellar mass and redshift.

### Research interests
- Supermassive black-hole growth and AGN demographics
- Black-hole–galaxy coevolution
- Galaxy mergers and AGN triggering
- Deep and wide multiwavelength surveys
- X-ray astronomy
- JWST, Euclid, Rubin/LSST, and Roman survey science
- Statistical inference and machine learning in astronomy
